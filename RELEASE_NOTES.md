# Casual Tinkering — 1.6.x

Two new tier 3 materials, each with its own identity and a set of traits you can build around.

## Quartziron

A dense iron-and-quartz alloy, made by casting molten quartz over iron parts or molten iron over quartz.

- **Sniping** — deals more damage the farther you are from your target. Works with both melee and ranged weapons.
- Works as arrow heads and thrown blades, where it also gains **Keen** for sharper projectiles.
- Can be made into armour plating and chainmail. Plating carries **Concealment**, which reduces incoming damage the farther the attacker is from you.
- Registered as a heavy ranged material, so its ranged traits apply where they should.

## Verdant Steel

A living green metal alloyed from molten copper, earth slime, and molten clay.

- **Overslime** — the metal repairs itself over time.
- **Oversiphon** — experience orbs you pick up are partly turned into overslime instead of experience, refilling that self-repair reserve.
- Its molten form can be poured. It flows sluggishly like slime and sets off a small explosion on contact that hurts creatures but breaks no blocks and starts no fires.
- Can be made into armour plating and chainmail.

## Both materials

- Each has its own ingot.
- Full part textures for tools, weapons, and armour.
- Dedicated encyclopedia entries covering how to make them and what they do.

## Fixes

- Thrown boomerangs no longer lose their damage after a world is saved and reloaded.
- Sniping now applies its melee damage bonus correctly, without the excessive knockback it previously caused.
- The Molten Verdant Steel encyclopedia entry now matches the wording of the other slime alloys.
