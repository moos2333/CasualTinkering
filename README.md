# Casual Tinkering

A Tinkers' Construct 2 addon that introduces new tools for combat and utility.

## Tools

**Circular Saw** — A heavy woodcutting tool with a spinning blade. Acts as an axe, and can be charged to unleash a wide sweeping attack that damages all enemies in a frontal cone.

**Magic Device** — A ranged magic weapon. Charge it to summon flying magic swords that deal armor-piercing damage, or hold it in your off-hand to assist your main-hand attacks.

## Modifiers

- **Precision Sawing** — Logs drop as planks when harvested with the Circular Saw.
- **Overclock** — Increases sweep attack damage against secondary targets.
- **Magic Lance** — Replaces the Magic Device's summoned swords with a piercing lance.
- **Auto Device** — Automatically strikes nearby hostile mobs.
- **Magic Membrane** — Provides damage reduction when the Magic Device is equipped in the chestplate slot.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.12.2
- Forge
- Tinkers' Construct 2
- Mantle
